A film camera for after dark

NOKTUR is a film camera for iPhone, with real dials, a focus ring and film recipes you can share.

Getting help

Found a problem or have a question? Open an issue on GitHub. Say which iPhone and iOS version you have, and what happened.

Good to know

  • NOKTUR runs on iPhone with iOS 17 or later, and is used in landscape.
  • To open it from the lock screen, Control Center, the Action button or Camera Control, add the NOKTUR control. This needs iOS 18.
  • 48 MP, ProRAW, ProRes and the extra lenses appear only on iPhones that have them.
  • To add a recipe someone shared, open RECIPE and point the camera at its card, or pick a picture of the card.
  • If NOKTUR can't use the camera, turn it on in Settings › NOKTUR › Camera.
  • Bought NOKTUR Pro on another iPhone? Open MENU › NOKTUR Pro › Restore Purchase.
  • Asking for help with NOKTUR Pro? Include the Support ID from MENU › Support ID.

Privacy

Your photos, recipes and settings stay on your iPhone. Read the privacy policy.
